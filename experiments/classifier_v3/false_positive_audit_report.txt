6. ANSWERS TO SPECIFIC DIAGNOSTIC QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Are the 50 false positives visually unusual?
A1: Yes, visually and statistically distinct. The false positives exhibit significantly
    lower contrast (overall std dev 33.25 vs 41.41, p=0.036), lower ridge structure variation
    (16.49 vs 20.48, p=0.0029), significantly lower Shannon entropy (5.21 vs 5.64, p<0.0001),
    and smaller foreground bounding box area (15,729 vs 18,475 px, p=0.0142). Visually, they are
    predominantly faint prints, partial prints with unprinted margins, or children's fingers with narrow ridge flow.

Q2: Is there evidence of an image-quality / domain-shift problem?
A2: Yes, very clear evidence. 50% of the false positives (25/50) are from the CHILD category,
    and 84% (42/50) are concentrated within just 5 specific families (FAMILY-36, FAMILY-20,
    FAMILY-78, FAMILY-54, FAMILY-65) whose fingerprint captures are consistently fainter, lighter,
    and smaller than the average adult print in the dataset.

Q3: Does the V3 synthetic clean distribution differ substantially from real clean?
A3: The V3 clean training samples are uniform crops that had full-sized, well-inked prints
    (mean foreground 31.4%), whereas the real test split contains faint, small, and partial
    prints that were under-represented among clean training samples.

Q4: Would a higher threshold materially reduce false positives while preserving acceptable recall?
A4: Yes. Raising the decision threshold from 0.48 to 0.65 cuts real clean false positives
    from 50 down to 23 (a 54% reduction) while maintaining Test Distorted Recall at 88.0%
    and Bending Recall at 88.0%. Raising to 0.70 cuts FPs to 18 while keeping Bending Recall at 84.0%.

Q5: What should we fix BEFORE the next training run?
A5: 1. Data Augmentation on Clean Samples: Incorporate intensity jitter, partial masking,
       and contrast variation so the classifier recognizes faint/partial clean prints as CLEAN.
    2. Calibrated Decision Threshold: Tune threshold taking into account specificity and clean
       generalization, not just synthetic validation F1.
    3. Hard Negative Mining: Expose the model during training to low-contrast and partial clean prints.
